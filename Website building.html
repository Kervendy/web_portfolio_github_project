About Me

I’m Kervendy Coicou, a father, husband, son, and lifelong learner. I grew up in Haiti, where family was everything and opportunities were hard to find. We showed up, worked hard, and waited for results. When I moved to America, I started over and rebuilt my path through education, persistence, and curiosity. Along the way, I fell in love with artificial intelligence. I use it every day as a learning partner to work through hard material, test my thinking, and turn rough ideas into finished work. I’m also learning to question its answers and to know where human judgment must stay in charge.
